Why Do We Need a General World Model?

Current video generation models simulate pixels, but do they understand the world? We propose that a true General World Model must be grounded in the Trinity of Consistency. It's not just about looking real—it's about aligning with semantic instructions, respecting 3D geometry, and obeying physical laws over time.

Text ↔ Vision

Modal Consistency

The Interface

The model must accurately translate semantic instructions (language) into visual manifestations, ensuring every described object and attribute appears as requested.

3D Geometry

Spatial Consistency

The Basis

The world is 3D. Objects must maintain their geometric structure, relative positions, and perspective correctness across different viewpoints.

Causality & Physics

Temporal Consistency

The Engine

Events must follow cause and effect. Gravity, collisions, and object permanence must hold true over time, avoiding "dream-like" morphing.
